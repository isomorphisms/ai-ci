For controls that claim to change rendered semantics, drive a deterministic input through an emulator or identified physical target, record the resulting semantic parameter/state receipt, and compare a rendered region that excludes the control overlay. Require a nontrivial downstream difference and bind the evidence to the exact build under review. Keep project-specific coordinates, thresholds, and visual invariants in the consumer repository rather than hard-coding them in ai-ci.
When an accelerated surface is absent from system/compositor capture, require evidence from the renderer's own output layer (for example a debug-only GLES framebuffer readback) rather than weakening the visual assertion. The probe must exercise the same renderer path and exclude control chrome; system screenshots may remain diagnostic evidence only.

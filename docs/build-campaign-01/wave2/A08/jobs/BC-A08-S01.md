# BC-A08-S01 — Independently accept the exact payload on its supported delivery surface

1. **Identity and execution:** SUN, Ultra; owner isomorphisms/ai-ci; parent BC-A08 under [BUILD-CAMPAIGN-01](https://github.com/isomorphisms/ai-ci/issues/239); canonical [issue 219](https://github.com/isomorphisms/ai-ci/issues/219).

2. **Outcome:** Delivery becomes accepted only when independent evidence binds the requested supported surface, exact complete payload and genuine transport observation. Retained bytes remain available when presentation fails.

3. **Recovered intent:** Issue 219 requires complete visible jobs. Its [October 9 record](https://github.com/isomorphisms/ai-ci/issues/219#issuecomment-6093384645) distinguishes user-confirmed plain-text/C67 success from missing retained exact bytes and undeployed generic sink. Unsupported Android CodeBlock presentation must fail even when composition succeeds. [Issue 226](https://github.com/isomorphisms/ai-ci/issues/226) rejects nonexistent final file links.

4. **Current evidence:** [AICI 949a60dad5e014cafce2c49619df9bbdbcfaafc0](https://github.com/isomorphisms/ai-ci/tree/949a60dad5e014cafce2c49619df9bbdbcfaafc0), `validators/job_delivery.pi` and `docs/job-delivery.md`, already check exact identity, attachment fields, stages and recovery; caller-captured data is not platform attestation. [isomorphisms/flexible-pipes PR #127, “Repair plain-text job delivery and invalidate recovered success”](https://github.com/isomorphisms/flexible-pipes/pull/127), head 266c4bd7d974a5d6348546b2c4429e3c67553433, already repairs plain text and resets recovered delivery while retaining accepted dispatch.

5. **Reuse:** Extend the existing independent validator and FP handoff v1/v2 interface. Preserve the existing 41-control family and reported FP controls. Do not recreate compose/materialize/deliver/recover, ZIP extraction or routing, and do not treat another fixture acknowledgement as a deployed sink.

6. **Scope and ownership:** AICI owns rejection and evidence-class distinction. BC-FP05 owns genuine text and attachment adapters, connector retrieval and dispatch. Retain separate composition, availability, transport, presentation, dispatch and execution states.

7. **Prerequisites:** Source/control work is READY against existing transport fixtures. Integration consumes separately released FP05 contract/payload digests, actual accepted bytes, message/file identity and observed capability; no whole-job dependency. Genuine acceptance waits for accessible trusted adapter/client evidence. Revalidate source, contract, audience, request authority and adapter identity after changes.

8. **Work:** Implement independent binding of adapter provenance and observed client capability to exact stage identity. Validate text completeness and attachment name/MIME/size/digest, archive provenance and unique member identity. Enforce display-only, display-and-dispatch and explicitly authorized dispatch-only independently. Consume FP recovery without promoting preserved dispatch into renewed presentation. Wire the validator as the actual pre-dispatch gate and retain raw rejected observations. Use existing checked Ithon conventions; prefer Idriç for any new helper.

9. **Acceptance:** Genuine complete plain text and an exact retrieved attachment are positive twins. Reject hidden-only payload, truncation, nonexistent path, one-byte mutation, wrong MIME/name, ambiguous ZIP member, forged sink, placeholder capability, unsupported component and premature dispatch. Benign wording outside the authoritative payload preserves identity. Replay identical observations deterministically; retry never duplicates dispatch and requires new delivery evidence. Adapter unavailability is BLOCKED, not validator PASS.

10. **Evidence limits:** Transport acknowledgement does not prove human presentation, installation, execution or physical outcome. Preserve the historical user-confirmed case without inventing its lost bytes.

11. **Completion and continuation:** Release the independently tested validator artifact before adapter integration, then retain one genuine text and one genuine attachment acceptance with rejecting twins. Publish exact outstanding presentation observations and update issue 219; escalate only contradictory platform semantics.

12. **Authority:** Implement and test reviewable candidates. No actual dispatch, external mutation, merge, closure, publication or device operation is granted by this job.

END OF BC-A08-S01
